# UX Audit — Golf Sync Swing 1.3.4 (1) — 2026-10-22

Full UX audit of the build that is live on the App Store: **3 P0, 11 P1, 14 P2, 10 P3.** Two passes
were merged, with duplicates folded together: a read of every user-facing view, view model and
service, and a runtime pass in the Simulator.

- **Audited tree:** `43d5d4f` on `analytics/repo-local-funnels`, which is what 1.3.4 (1) was built
  from. It is not on `main` until PR #13 merges. `file:line` references are relative to
  `golf-sync-swing/` in that tree; "pbxproj" means `golf-sync-swing.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj`.
- **Runtime pass:** Debug build with analytics switched off (no events sent), temporary iPhone 17
  and iPhone SE (3rd gen) simulators, iOS 26.1, live RevenueCat offerings, no purchase or restore.
  Passes: first launch, free, premium (debug override), Dark Mode, largest accessibility text size,
  German, small screen, camera and microphone denied, landscape.
- **Not reachable in the Simulator:** recording, countdown, detection, review and save (so the
  library cap at save), the pose overlay, a real purchase or restore, Customer Center, export with
  Photos denied, Mail. VoiceOver findings come from the accessibility tree plus code; VoiceOver
  itself was not run.
- **Live prices seen:** Lifetime $79.99; Annual $29.99/yr with a 1-week trial; Weekly $4.99/wk with
  a 3-day trial.

Severity: **P0** hurts a payer or invites an App Review rejection today. **P1** blocks activation or
conversion, or breaks a flow on a supported device. **P2** is friction or accessibility with real
impact. **P3** is polish.

## P0

### P0-1 A paid purchase sent the app into a launch crash loop for 48 h — fixed in PR #13, not released

- **What happened:** a Lifetime buyer (2026-10-15) was granted the entitlement by RevenueCat, then
  the app crashed within seconds of every launch for about 48 h. A 1★ "Golpe" review followed.
- **Most likely cause:** `Services/SubscriptionRevenueReporter.swift` (shipped in 1.3.3). On launch
  it handled every paid, standard-period charge it had not yet reported, and it stored its dedup
  date only after the StoreKit lookup and the Amplitude call. A crash inside it therefore repeats
  on every launch.
- **Not confirmed:** the crashing frame. Xcode → Organizer → Crashes → 1.3.3 / 1.3.4 has it.
- **Evidence:** `docs/prod-errors-2026-10-21.md` (in PR #13).
- **Fix:** PR #13 deletes the reporter; ship it as 1.3.5.

### P0-2 A purchase "succeeds" without checking the entitlement

**Where:** paywall, Settings.

- **Evidence:**
  - `Views/Paywall/Model/PaywallViewModel.swift:91-94`: `if result.userCancelled { return .cancelled }`
    → `refreshStatus()` → `.succeeded`. There is no `entitlements[…].isActive` check.
  - `CustomPaywallView.swift:198-201`: success only tracks the event and dismisses; nothing confirms
    the purchase.
  - Restore with an inactive entitlement says "No previous purchases found."
    (`PaywallViewModel.swift:120-126`, `SettingsView.swift:185-188`).
  - Every purchase error is "Couldn't complete purchase." (`PaywallViewModel.swift:95-101`),
    including a deferred Ask to Buy.
  - Manage Subscription (Customer Center) appears only for premium users
    (`SettingsView.swift:52-57`).
- **Impact:** if the entitlement ever fails to activate (receipt sync delay, offering or dashboard
  mistake), the charge goes through, the sheet closes and the feature stays locked. Buying again
  hits Apple's "already purchased" and closes silently. Restore contradicts the App Store, and
  there is no in-app route to help. Even a normal purchase ends with no confirmation, and the user
  has to repeat the action that opened the paywall. A child waiting for Ask to Buy approval is
  told the purchase failed. (This did not cause the Lifetime incident; it is the gap that would
  turn any activation hiccup into the same complaint.)
- **Fix:**
  - Require `result.customerInfo.entitlements[PurchaseService.entitlementID]?.isActive`.
  - If it is false, show "Activating…" and retry `syncPurchases()` / a fresh `customerInfo()` a
    few times. Then show "Payment received, Premium didn't activate", with Restore and Contact
    support.
  - On success, show "Premium unlocked" with a haptic, then resume the gated action.
  - Show a pending payment as "Waiting for approval".
  - On Restore, if `allPurchasedProductIdentifiers` is non-empty but the entitlement is inactive,
    say exactly that and offer support.
  - Offer "Trouble with a purchase?" to non-premium users too.
- **Effort:** M.

### P0-3 Terms and Privacy open WithCoach's documents

**Where:** paywall footer, Settings → Legal.

- **Evidence:** `Views/Paywall/Components/PaywallFooter.swift:15-16` (`withcoach.app`) and
  `Views/Settings/SettingsView.swift:98-99` (`www.withcoach.app`). Both pages, fetched 2026-10-21,
  describe the WithCoach app and its account registration. They never mention golf, swings,
  camera or video, and the terms carry no EULA wording.
- **Impact:**
  - Guideline 3.1.2 requires a working Terms of Use / EULA link for auto-renewing subscriptions.
  - Guideline 5.1.1(i) requires a privacy policy that covers this app's data: camera video,
    Amplitude, RevenueCat, MetricKit.
  - EU users are owed the same transparency under GDPR.
  - 1.3.4 passed review with these links, so rejection isn't certain, but any reviewer who taps
    them sees another app.
- **Fix:**
  - Terms → Apple's standard EULA (`https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/`)
    or an app-specific page.
  - Publish a Golf Sync Swing privacy policy.
  - Use one shared constant for both screens, and put the same privacy URL in App Store Connect.
- **Effort:** S for the code, plus writing the pages.

## P1

### P1-1 Paywall copy contradicts the prices and the product

- **Evidence:**
  - The Annual card shows a computed "BEST VALUE · SAVE 88%" (`PaywallPlan.swift:106-123`) next to
    a hard-coded "Save 81% vs weekly" (`PaywallPlan.swift:61`; "81" is in all 12 translations).
    81% matches a $49.99 annual price, 88% the live $29.99. Seen on iPhone 17, SE, in German and
    in landscape.
  - Opened from Settings, the headline "Go Pro. Every feature. Forever." sits above a preselected
    renewing Annual plan (`PaywallHero.swift:71-72`).
  - "No watermark, multi-aspect" (`PaywallFeatureList.swift:38`): no export is ever watermarked
    (`exportNoWatermark` is unused, `FeatureAccess.swift:15`), and the aspect choice is free.
  - "8× slow-mo" (`PaywallFeatureList.swift:32`): 0.125× is free for everyone.
  - The `poseEstimation` gate is unused; the skeleton overlay is free.
  - The real gates go unmentioned: more than 3 recordings, comparing new swings with pros,
    Stacked/Sequential modes, HD export.
  - "onion-skin, overlay" both mean the Stacked mode; Russian reads "наложение, оверлей".
- **Impact:** a wrong savings claim in every storefront, and people pay for things they already
  have. Refund, 1★ and 3.1.2 risk.
- **Fix:**
  - Build the savings line from `savingsPercent`, or drop it.
  - Say "Forever" only when Lifetime is selected.
  - Rewrite the rows around the real gates, using the mode names the app shows.
  - Drop "no watermark", or add a free-tier watermark (product decision; see P2-5).
- **Effort:** S.

### P1-2 Trial terms are thin, and Restore / Terms / Privacy are out of reach

- **Evidence:**
  - The button says "Start 1-week free trial" with only "Cancel anytime." under it
    (`PaywallCTA.swift:59-80`). Nothing says "then $29.99/year, renews automatically".
  - The footer sits at the end of the scroll content, below the pinned plans and button
    (`CustomPaywallView.swift:64-79`). It is 13 pt at about 40% white (≈3.4–3.8:1).
  - **iPhone SE:** the pinned block covers about 55% of the screen.
  - **Largest text size:** the pinned block fills the screen. Hero, features, Restore, Terms and
    Privacy are all hidden, the close button overlaps the Lifetime card, plan descriptions are cut
    off, and prices stay at a fixed 15 pt (`PaywallPlanPicker.swift:97`).
  - **Landscape on SE:** the same.
- **Impact:** users can't restore or read the terms at the moment of purchase. That is a compliance
  problem and an accessibility problem, and users who miss the post-trial charge are the ones
  who ask for refunds.
- **Fix:**
  - Under the button, built from the selected plan: "1 week free, then $29.99/year. Renews
    automatically until cancelled." For Lifetime: "One-time payment of $79.99."
  - A Restore · Terms · Privacy row inside the pinned block, with 44 pt targets and ≥4.5:1 contrast.
  - At accessibility sizes or on short screens, move the plan list into the scroll view and keep
    only the button pinned. Let prices scale.
- **Effort:** S/M.

### P1-3 Permission prompts are inaccurate and English-only, and the app uses three names

- **Evidence:**
  - The microphone text (pbxproj:466/508) says it detects "ball impact sounds for automatic swing
    detection". Detection is video-only; audio is simply recorded into the movie
    (`CaptureSessionConfigurator.swift:14`: "deliberately NO audio DATA output").
  - The photo-library text (pbxproj:468/510) promises an import that doesn't exist (P1-8).
  - There is no `InfoPlist.xcstrings`, so all three prompts are English in all 12 locales.
  - Names: "AI Golf Swing Sync" on the home screen (pbxproj:463), "Golf Sync Swing" in prompts
    (`CameraPermissionGate.swift:71`), "GOLF SYNC PRO" on the paywall (`PaywallHero.swift:30`).
- **Impact:** 5.1.1 expects purpose strings to describe the real use. Non-English users meet an
  English prompt at the moment they are most likely to deny it, and a camera denial means no value.
- **Fix:**
  - Microphone: "…to record sound with your swing videos."
  - Drop `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` until import ships (PhotosPicker needs none).
  - Add `InfoPlist.xcstrings` for the 12 locales.
  - Pick one name.
- **Effort:** S.

### P1-4 Landscape breaks onboarding and the paywall

- **Evidence:** pbxproj:473/515 allow landscape on iPhone, and no screen adapts. Seen on SE:
  - **Onboarding:** Skip and Continue are off-screen, and a swipe doesn't advance. The user is
    stuck until they rotate back.
  - **Permission page:** the explanation is cut to one line.
  - **Camera "How It Works":** "Preparing camera…" overlaps "Match this pose".
  - **Compare:** the fixed header takes about 40% of the height, and the tab bar covers the pro
    cards.
  - **Paywall:** Restore, Terms and Privacy can't be reached, and the close button overlaps the
    Lifetime card.
- **Impact:** golfers mount phones sideways. App Review also runs iPhone apps on iPad, where a
  landscape reviewer hits an onboarding with no buttons.
- **Fix:** portrait only on iPhone, in both configurations. Allow landscape later, per screen, only
  for the players and the comparison.
- **Effort:** S.

### P1-5 Comparison export is impossible on 4.7-inch iPhones

- **Evidence:**
  - `ExportProgressView.swift:64` sets `.presentationDetents([.medium])`, and the content is a
    plain VStack with no ScrollView (`:49`, `:92-108`).
  - **SE:** "Export Video" sits at y=684–734 on a 667 pt screen, and dragging doesn't enlarge the
    sheet. The 60 pt film icon (`:94-95`) overlaps the title, and the Trim subtitle is cut off.
  - **iPhone 17:** the same icon overlap, with the button clipped at the sheet edge.
  - The single-video export sheet fits, even on SE.
  - **Labels:**
    - The headline reads "Export at Vertical (TikTok) (720×1 280)": double brackets and a digit
      group separator (`:136`, `:394-396`).
    - "Recommended" also shows on the locked HD row.
    - 1440×2560 is called "Full HD".
    - Premium users default to Standard (`:42`).
  - **Flow:** two Export taps (`ExportEditorView.swift:90-101` → this sheet), and Cancel discards
    the framing done in the editor.
- **Impact:** owners of SE 2nd and 3rd gen (both run iOS 26) can't export a comparison at all, and
  export is a headline feature.
- **Fix:**
  - `.presentationDetents([.medium, .large])` plus a ScrollView, or pin the button with
    `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`. Shrink the icon.
  - "Vertical · 720×1280"; name the tiers 720p, 1080p and 1440p; preselect the best unlocked tier.
  - Later: move quality and trim into the editor so export is one tap, and let Cancel return to
    the editor.
- **Effort:** S for the layout, M to merge the steps.

### P1-6 No "Compare with a pro" after the first save

- **Evidence:** after saving, `RecordingView.swift:577` opens the History detail. Its controls are
  back, the mode menu, export and EDIT/ADD (`PlayerTopBarView.swift:17-27`), with no compare entry.
  The free comparison is only a caption on another tab (`HomeView.swift:109-115`).
- **Impact:** 1.3.4's activation bet (the first pro comparison is free) needs five unprompted taps
  across a tab switch.
- **Fix:** after the first save, show a primary "Compare with <pro> →" on the History detail. Pick
  the pro by club and open the comparison directly. Show a "Free" badge on pro cards while the free
  comparison is unused.
- **Effort:** S–M.

### P1-7 Locks are invisible until tapped, and every lock shows the same paywall

- **Evidence:**
  - All gates pass `.featureGate` (`RecordingView.swift:148`, `HomeView.swift:52`,
    `ComparisonView.swift:52`, `ExportProgressView.swift:66`), so all get "Unlock the full
    comparison." (`PaywallHero.swift:69-70`, `:80-81`).
  - Two pros can be selected and "Compare 2 Swings" is enabled. Tapping it opens the paywall, and
    closing the paywall returns to the same selection, so the button loops.
  - **Library cap:** the 4th save is blocked only after recording (`RecordingViewModel.swift:1073-1078`;
    the limit is 3 recordings, `LibraryGateService.swift:14,19`). It shows the comparison paywall,
    and Save loops back to it. No counter appears anywhere. The comment at
    `RecordingView.swift:88-90` promises copy about freeing a slot in History; that copy doesn't
    exist.
  - The caption "Compare your first video with a pro for free…" stays after the free comparison
    is used.
- **Impact:** a user saving recording #4 sees a paywall about comparison modes. They never learn the
  limit or the free way out, which is to delete an old recording.
- **Fix:**
  - Pass the gate reason into the paywall: library full, pro comparison, mode or HD. Show a
    matching headline, e.g. "Library full — 3 of 3 free recordings", plus "Free up space in
    History".
  - Show "2 of 3 free recordings" on the Camera and History screens before the cap.
  - Show lock state while selecting ("Pick one of your swings + one pro").
  - Clear a locked selection when the paywall closes.
  - Update the caption once the free comparison is spent.
  - Add the reason to `paywall_shown`.
- **Effort:** M.

### P1-8 No path to value without recording

- **Evidence:**
  - Import has no entry point: `VideoPickerView`, `VideoLibraryView` and
    `VideoImportService.importVideo` have zero call sites.
  - Three places promise it anyway: `HistoryView.swift:26` ("Import videos to start marking
    swings"), `HomeView.swift:29` ("Record or import a video to get started"), and the
    photo-library permission text.
  - Pro-vs-pro is never free (`FirstComparisonAccess.swift:15`).
  - A user who denied the camera has no way in.
  - On a fresh install, Compare shows "No Swings Yet" for 10–15 s, with no loading state, before
    the 19 pros appear (`HomeView.swift:25-31` decides on counts only).
  - In production, main_app → recording_started was 57% (funnel of 2026-08-01). About 43% never
    record.
- **Fix:**
  - **Now:** empty states with real actions ("Record your first swing" → Camera tab), plus
    placeholder pro cards while the library loads.
  - **Next:** Import from Photos (PhotosPicker, videos only), running through the same detection
    and counting toward the library cap. PR #12 already put a file-based detector on `main`
    (`VideoFileSwingDetector`, `VideoFileFrameReader`, debug-only), which is the part import needs.
  - A free pro-vs-pro demo is a product decision.
- **Effort:** S for copy, actions and loading; M for import.

### P1-9 Sync can't be corrected where it is judged

- **Evidence:**
  - The offset comes only from the two impact markers (`ComparisonViewModel.swift:37`).
  - The comparison has no nudge and no impact tick (`ComparisonView.swift:110-118`). With
    mis-marked swings, the pro sits at address while you're at the top of the backswing, and
    nothing on that screen can fix it.
  - The only path is History → player → EDIT, into a sheet titled "Edit Recording", then dragging
    "Contact" (`SwingEditorSheet.swift:97-110`).
  - The marker handles are 8 pt wide (`SwingMarkerSlider.swift:47-73`, `:88-91`), with no frame
    stepping and no VoiceOver support.
  - Pro markers can't be edited at all.
  - "Delete Swing" deletes at once (`SwingEditorSheet.swift:181-188`).
- **Impact:** a heuristic miss of a few frames breaks "frame-locked at impact", and the user can't
  fix it where they see it.
- **Fix:**
  - "Adjust sync" in the comparison: ±1 frame on the second clip plus dragging, saved per pair,
    with "Reset to auto".
  - An impact tick on the timeline and "Jump to impact".
  - 44 pt hit areas on the handles, adjustable with VoiceOver.
  - Rename the sheet "Edit swing markers", and confirm before deleting.
- **Effort:** M.

### P1-10 The first comparison shows both swings tiny

- **Evidence:**
  - Side-by-side puts a portrait user clip next to a 3:2 landscape pro clip. Each is about 200 pt
    wide, about 60% of the stage is black, and the pro clip touches the screen edge.
  - There are no You / pro-name labels.
  - The canvas width is the sum of both clips, split in equal halves
    (`ComparisonLayoutStrategy.swift:42-56`).
  - The mode resets to Side-by-Side every time (`ComparisonViewModel.swift:39`).
- **Impact:** this is the activation "aha" moment, and it looks poor.
- **Fix:**
  - When the orientations differ, default to Top/Bottom, or crop both to one portrait frame around
    the golfer.
  - Add small labels.
  - Remember the last mode.
- **Effort:** M.

### P1-11 Support is a Gmail mailto in Settings only

- **Evidence:**
  - `SettingsView.swift:86-90` opens `mailto:examply.app@gmail.com`, with an empty body: no

  - Without a Mail account, the tap does nothing visible.

    product and iOS version. Fall back to a copy-address alert.
- **Effort:** S.

## P2

### P2-1 Recording is hard to use from 3 m away

- **Evidence:**
  - The countdown is fixed at 5 s (`RecordingViewModel.swift:854`).
  - "Swing detected" is a haptic plus a green flash (`:267`). There are no sounds anywhere.
  - The front camera at 30 fps is hard-coded (`RecordingView.swift:474/477`,
    `RecordingViewModel.swift:917`).
  - The "How It Works" overlay dims the preview and can't be dismissed
    (`PositioningGuideOverlay.swift:53-59`, `RecordingView.swift:265-268`).
- **Fix:**
  - A 3/5/10 s countdown.
  - Tick beeps, a start chime and a "swing captured" sound.
  - Collapse the guide after first use.
  - A rear-camera / 60–120 fps option.
  - Optional auto-stop after N swings.
- **Effort:** M.

### P2-2 "No swing detected" gives no reason and no way forward

- **Evidence:**
  - The message is a bare "We couldn't find a swing in this take…" (`RecordingViewModel.swift:1262`).
  - `CameraTipsOverlay` and `RecordingTipsSheet` are used only in previews.
  - Compare shows a plain "No swings detected" (`SwingSelectionListView.swift:39-42`).
- **Fix:** list the likely causes (full body in frame, 2.5–3.5 m away, backlight), link the tips,

- **Effort:** S.

### P2-4 Destructive actions without confirmation

### P2-5 Exports are fragile and don't travel well

- **Evidence:**
  - **Photos denied:** the user sees "Export Failed … Try Again" although the encode worked
    (`ExportProgressView.swift:226-246`, `:362-370`).
  - **Backgrounding:** exported files are deleted every time the app goes to the background

  - **After Save:** the file is deleted and Share disappears (`VideoExportService.swift:331`,

  - **Single-video export:**
    - It has no Share option (`SingleVideoExportSheet.swift:106-117`).
    - It asks for Photos only after encoding.

  - **The file itself:**
    - It is named `export_<UUID>.mp4` (`VideoExportService.swift:317-322`).

- **Fix:**
  - Separate "Saved" and "Couldn't save to Photos — Open Settings" states.
  - Keep the file until the sheet closes, and request background time.
  - Keep Share after Save, and add Share to single-video export.
  - Ask for Photos before encoding.
  - Readable names like `GolfSync-2026-10-22-vs-Minjee-Lee.mp4`.
  - Subtle labels plus a small app mark for free users (product decision; it would make "no
    watermark" a real Pro perk).
- **Effort:** M.

### P2-7 Large text and low contrast break the main screens

- **Evidence:**
  - **Fixed `.system(size:)` text:**

## What works well

- **Paywall:**
  - The close button is visible immediately and is disabled while a purchase is in flight.
  - Prices and trial eligibility come live from RevenueCat, so ineligible users are never promised
    a trial (`PaywallViewModel.swift:146-171`).
  - The German copy fits.
- **Free-comparison rules are correct.**
  - Allowed: your swing + a pro, and the same swing + another pro.
  - Locked: a second own swing, or pro + pro.
- **Comparison core:** playback stays in sync, with swap, labelled frame stepping, 0.125–1× speeds,
  locked modes marked "(Pro)", and an error overlay with Try Again.
- **Camera permission recovery:**
  - Not-asked, denied and restricted are handled separately.
  - Open Settings works, and the state is re-checked on return.
- **Recording is honest:**
  - A hung finalize recovers into review.
  - Takes with no swing are kept.
  - An interruption shows an overlay with Resume.
  - Free space is checked (500 MB).
  - Camera start-up has a time limit and a retry.
- **History:** each row is one labelled button. Swipe-to-delete asks for confirmation and has a
  VoiceOver action.
- **Export on 6.1-inch and larger screens:** works end to end, with aspect presets, progress, Save
  to Photos and Share.
- **Localization:** 12 complete locales (288 keys, one gap), good translator comments, metric units
  in the translations.
