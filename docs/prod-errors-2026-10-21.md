# Production Errors — golf-sync-swing — 2026-10-21

Generated by the `prod-errors` skill, scoped to one incident: a customer bought Lifetime and
reported that they never got access. Production build: **1.3.4 (1)**, READY_FOR_SALE, built from
`analytics/repo-local-funnels` (not merged to `main`).

---

## Verdict

**The purchase was granted correctly; the app crashed.** RevenueCat recorded the Lifetime sale
(2026-10-15 14:45 UTC, Brazil, BRL 499.90, offering `default`) with
`entitlement_ids: ["Golf Swing Sync Premium"]`, the exact entitlement the app checks. The dashboard
configuration is correct. On the device, the purchase screen never reported success
(`paywall_purchased` never arrived), and from 14:46:04 the app crashed within 1–3 s of every launch:
more than ten launches in half a minute, each followed by a MetricKit `crash_detected`, and again
on Oct 16. The buyer's Oct 20 session, past the 48-hour mark, ran normally. The 1★
review "Golpe" ("scam", BRA, Oct 16) is almost certainly this buyer.

The only launch-time code that runs only in the 48 h after a paid, non-trial purchase is
`SubscriptionRevenueReporter.reportIfNeeded → report()`. It stored its dedup date only after the
StoreKit product lookup and the Amplitude revenue call, so a crash anywhere inside it repeats on
every launch until the window closes. Trials never enter it, and this was the first non-trial sale
in production. **The exact crashing frame is not confirmed** — see the manual step below.

Fix on branch `fix/lifetime-purchase-crash-loop`: the client-side reporter is removed; recognised
revenue already reaches Amplitude from RevenueCat's server-side integration (`rc_*` events).

## Findings

1. **Launch crash loop for 48 h after a paid purchase (P0, money).** Evidence as above. Every paid,
   non-trial purchase (Lifetime, or a subscription bought without a trial) is exposed in 1.3.3 and
   1.3.4; the reporter shipped in 1.3.3.
2. **The promotional grant may reopen the window.** It gives the entitlement a new
   `latestPurchaseDate` (Oct 20 16:57 UTC). Its product id has no App Store product, so the
   lookup probably returns nothing and exits early, but that is unverified.
3. **Revenue analytics never worked client-side.** No `purchase_completed`, `trial_started` or
   `paywall_purchased` had ever been ingested as of the 2026-10-01 funnel report; RevenueCat's
   server-side `rc_*` events are the working revenue source.
